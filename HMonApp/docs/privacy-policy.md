## 1. Who we are

HMon is an educational mobile application used by schools participating in the Erasmus+ project “Hydroponics, AI and IoT for Sustainable Education” (HAI). It helps teachers and students collect experiment data from hydroponic growing systems.

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how the school configures the app, HMon may process:

- **Experiment metadata:** school name, country, system ID, plant cultivar, experiment dates  
- **IoT / sensor readings:** pH, electrical conductivity (EC), TDS, water and air temperature, humidity, water-level status  
- **Phenotyping measurements:** plant height, stem length, leaf number, leaf area, shoot number for plants labelled P1–P10  
- **Lighting logs:** light type, daily duration, light–plant distance, zone light levels, observed plant issues  
- **Photographs:** overview and close-up plant images uploaded for educational analysis  
- **Technical data:** anonymous authentication tokens when cloud sync (Firebase) is enabled  

The app does **not** require personal student accounts. Avoid entering personal names, emails, or photos of identifiable people.

## 3. Why we process data

- To run and document the school hydroponics experiment  
- To show growth reports and educational recommendations inside the app  
- To enable optional AI / image analysis (for example leaf-area estimation)  
- To allow cross-school / cross-country comparison within the HAI project when cloud sync is enabled  

## 4. Where data is stored

- **On the device** when cloud sync is not configured (demo / offline mode)  
- **In Google Firebase** (Firestore and Cloud Storage) when the school administrator configures a Firebase project  

Data may be accessed by authorised project partners in participating countries for educational and research purposes related to HAI.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Aggregated or experiment datasets may be shared among HAI partner schools and published in educational reports. Photos should show plants and equipment only.

## 6. Retention

Schools and project coordinators decide how long experiment data is kept. Device data can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage. Cloud data is retained according to the project’s Firebase project settings.

## 7. Children’s use

HMon is intended for use in supervised school settings. Teachers/guardians should ensure no unnecessary personal information is entered.
